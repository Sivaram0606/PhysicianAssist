The Clinical Bottleneck

Why Traditional Intake & Pre-Visit Prep Break Down

Clinicians are data-rich yet time-poor—spending hours manually bridging static questionnaires and clinical documentation.

1

Physician "Pajama Time"

  • Hours spent after clinic synthesizing scattered registry JSONs, long intake forms, and lab PDFs.
  • Manual drafting of HPI and ROS notes drives clinician burnout and administrative fatigue.
2

Zero Questionnaire Tailoring

  • Coordinators manually pick from static departmental form catalogs.
  • Templates cannot adapt on-the-fly to a patient's active comorbidities, labs, or medication risks.
3

Survey Fatigue & Dropouts

  • Patients receive lengthy, generic surveys packed with redundant or non-applicable questions.
  • Leads to low completion rates, missing pre-visit safety signals, and unfocused clinic time.

Solution Overview

One AI Assistant, Three Core Clinical Capabilities

Turning high-volume patient data into verified, clinician-controlled action across the care continuum.

1

Patient Narratives

2

Physician Notes

What it does: Drafts 8-domain structured clinical notes plus ambient hematopathology/oncology lab PDF interpretations.

✓ Ready for rapid physician review, audit inspection, and sign-off.

3

Next-Visit Questionnaires

What it does: Tailors personalized follow-up surveys (Retained, Dropped, AI-Suggested) and powers a conversational intake bot.

✓ Helps both physicians prepare and patients respond via voice or text.
